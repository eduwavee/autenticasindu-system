## Scope
Whole PWA (index.html + src/). Mode: Operate. Single user: the shop owner, on her phone, at the counter and at home.

## Task
Charge a sale in seconds; read today's income and what clients owe at a glance; manage stock by size/color; record expenses; share catalog by WhatsApp.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The boutique back-room, not an accounting app. Refuses the grey POS with a pink accent: pink owns the shell and garments behave like garments.
OWN-WORLD: Fusion chosen by user of "El Perchero" (assigned) + "Etiqueta colgante" (pick). Ground white with faint blush tint; deep rose (#b0265f-ish) top bar and active nav; soft pink panels; ink plum-black text. One sans family (system/Figtree-like workhorse), tabular numerals. Hang-tag shape (clipped top corners + eyelet hole + string) is the container for products and the sale ticket; the perchero (a rail with one hanger per size, count below, empty hook dashed) is the stock display.
STORY: She opens the app, sees today's caja (cobrado, por medio de pago, fiado pendiente), taps the big Vender button, picks garments by tag, chooses size on the perchero, charges, shares the ticket by WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Rose top bar with "Auténticas" wordmark and date. Below, a large hang-tag card "Hoy" with total cobrado in big tabular figures, ventas count and ticket promedio; a strip of payment-method split; "Te deben" and stock-bajo chips. Bottom tab bar (Inicio, Vender, Stock, Clientas, Más) with a raised rose Vender button in the thumb zone.
FORM: Fusion of grounded #5 (perchero, assigned) and #1 (hang tag, pick); seed key 477cc6d3 (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature move: size rail of hangers for stock and variant picking.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
